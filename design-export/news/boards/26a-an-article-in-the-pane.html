Fire burns 400 acres in the foothills; evacuation warnings issued

imageNBC Los Angeles

A brush fire burning in the hills above the 210 Freeway grew to about 400 acres overnight, fire officials said Wednesday morning, as crews worked to keep it from pushing toward homes in the foothills.

Evacuation warnings were issued late Tuesday for neighborhoods north of the freeway. No homes had been damaged as of 7 a.m., and no injuries were reported.

Firefighters held the fire's western edge overnight with help from water-dropping helicopters. Containment stood at 10%.

A Red Flag Warning takes effect at noon and runs through Thursday evening, with gusts up to 50 mph and humidity below 10% expected across the valleys.

Two schools inside the warning area closed for the day. Officials asked residents to keep their vehicles fueled and to sign up for the county's emergency alerts.
